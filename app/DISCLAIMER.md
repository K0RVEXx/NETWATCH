## What NETWATCH does and does not do

- It reads the list of network connections of the computer it runs on (like `netstat`).
  It does **not** capture, read or store the content of your traffic.
- To show locations, it sends **remote IP addresses** to the free ip-api.com service.
  Private and local addresses are never sent. This can be turned off in Settings.
- It resolves host names with your normal DNS (can be turned off).
- Alerts are hints, not proof. A "new app" or "new country" alert is usually harmless
  (updates, CDNs, game servers). NETWATCH is **not an antivirus** and does not replace one.
- Location data from IP addresses is approximate.
